This is special project for Capri2 and Capri3. 
Due to Capri 2 doesn't provide MB FRU data during manifucturing so that the implement of this project will force to update MB FRU to Capri 2. 
You shall make sure you use this firmware image either on Capri2 or Capri3.

Common targets are:
    obmc-phosphor-image
